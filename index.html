The Chick-fil-A Story

Chick-fil-A traces its roots to 1946, when founder S. Truett Cathy opened the Dwarf Grill in Hapeville, Georgia. Truett went on to create the Original Chicken Sandwich and opened the first Chick-fil-A restaurant in Atlanta in 1967. The company remains family owned, famously closes on Sundays so Team Members can rest, and has grown to thousands of restaurants across North America. Chick-fil-A arrived in Canada in 2019, and in 2023 our restaurant proudly brought the brand to London, Ontario.

Our Story

Chick-fil-A Wharncliffe & Wonderland opened its doors on November 30, 2023, bringing the first Chick-fil-A restaurant to London, Ontario. Located in The District at 3459 Wonderland Road South, we opened with a team of roughly 130 full and part time Team Members, and we have been serving our neighbours with signature hospitality ever since. From Dotsy and Bingo nights to fundraiser evenings with local organizations, our restaurant is built to be a gathering place for the community we love.
